01
Nutrient Bioavailability

The label says 30g of protein. But how much does your body actually absorb? It depends on the food's molecular structure, how it was processed, and what else you ate with it. Oscar designs nutrition around what your body uses — not what's printed on the package.

Bioavailability · Amino acid absorption
02
The Gut–Muscle Connection

Your gut microbiome directly controls how fast you recover, how much energy you have, and how effectively your muscles rebuild. Most trainers have never studied this. Oscar built a significant part of his career understanding it — and it shows in every program he writes.

Microbiome · Inflammatory response
03
Anti-Inflammatory Protocols

Chronic low-grade inflammation is the silent enemy of results — especially after 40. It slows recovery, disrupts hormones, and makes you feel like training isn't working. Oscar structures both exercise and nutrition to actively reduce it, not ignore it.

Oxidative stress · Cortisol management
04
Supplement Integrity

The supplement market runs on claims that don't hold up scientifically. Oscar can evaluate the chemical stability of a product, how it behaves inside your body, and whether it's actually doing anything. He only recommends what the science backs — and the exact way to take it so it works.

Chemical stability · Efficacy analysis
